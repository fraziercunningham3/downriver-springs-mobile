---
name: Customer-sourced diagnostics
description: Product rule for inspection and diagnostic content in Downriver Springs
---

Inspection and diagnostic information must come from an identified real user, customer, or qualified technician. Uploaded media may be stored and shared, but the app must not infer or fabricate findings, confidence, severity, or repair recommendations without a real analysis source.

**Why:** Fabricated vehicle diagnoses could mislead customers and undermine the app's trust promise.

**How to apply:** Require a customer observation or technician-authored note for inspection records; label media-only entries as awaiting professional review; keep automated vision output disabled until a real, verified pipeline is connected.
